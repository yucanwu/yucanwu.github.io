Overview

The Hero Robot project focused on a competition-ready large-projectile platform with tightly integrated mechanisms, cameras, electronics, and maintainable packaging.

My contribution

  • Designed and assembled the 42 mm gooseneck ammunition-feeding mechanism.
  • Designed the feed-to-launch transition and six-friction-wheel launching system.
  • Developed a gravity-balancing mechanism for the gimbal pitch axis.
  • Integrated a movable video-transmission camera and a fixed industrial camera for autonomous aiming.
  • Designed the central electronics compartment and quick-release protective enclosure.
  • Planned cable routing and performed wiring, connector integration, and soldering.
  • Used SolidWorks for CAD, motion, and mechanical simulation; parts were fabricated with 3D printing, carbon-fiber plates, and CNC-machined aluminum.
  • As Vice Captain, helped recruit control/electronics members and trained newer mechanical and hardware members.
