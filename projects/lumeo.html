Overview

Lumeo is a campus autonomous-delivery robot project intended for closed or semi-closed environments, with indoor and outdoor operation. The current autonomous-driving prototype and robotic-arm control stack are at an early functional stage while full autonomous task execution and upper-level planning remain under development.

My contribution

  • Founded the team and served as project lead, technical lead, and robotic-arm lead.
  • Defined the overall vehicle technical route, sensor selection, and major component choices.
  • Designed the vehicle mechanical architecture and guided chassis iterations.
  • Designed and iterated the 5-DOF arm: yaw + 200 mm prismatic lift + three pitch joints.
  • Planned the control stack around Orin Nano + Linux + ROS 2 and DM-MC02/STM32 for real-time execution and safety.
  • Implemented Python/ROS 2 motor control for three pitch joints over SocketCAN, including protocol encapsulation, state parsing, position/velocity control, direction correction, reduction-ratio conversion, software zeroing, soft limits, and YAML configuration.
  • Built ROS 2 workspace/package/node/topic/service/launch/YAML infrastructure and completed three-motor integration testing.
  • Contributed to wiring, soldering, cable routing, and hardware integration.
