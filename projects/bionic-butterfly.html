Bio-Inspired Robotic Butterfly

A lightweight bio-inspired mechanism developed for the 31st Zhejiang University Student Mechanical Design Competition, where the project received Second Prize.

Bio-Inspired DesignSolidWorks3D PrintingCarbon Rod

My contribution

  • Designed the overall butterfly structure.
  • Completed 3D modeling and mechanical simulation in SolidWorks.
  • Assembled and integrated the mechanical and electronic systems.
  • Used 3D-printed parts, custom carbon rods, and commercial hardware.

Competition result

The project was submitted by team Jiangnan Machinery Factory and received Second Prize in the 31st Zhejiang University Student Mechanical Design Competition.
