Bio-Inspired Robotic Butterfly

A lightweight flapping-wing prototype developed for a mechanical design competition. I focused on overall structural design, SolidWorks modeling, and mechanical/electronic integration from CAD to a working prototype.

Bio-Inspired Design SolidWorks 3D Printing Carbon Rod Electromechanical Integration

Project overview

The project focused on translating a bio-inspired flapping concept into a lightweight physical prototype under competition constraints. The final design combined a carbon-rod frame, servo-driven front wings, passively coupled rear wings, and compact embedded control for remote flight testing.

My contribution

  • Designed the overall butterfly structure and mechanical layout.
  • Completed 3D modeling and mechanical simulation in SolidWorks.
  • Assembled and integrated the mechanical and electronic systems.

Mechanical concept

To keep the prototype lightweight, the mechanism used actively driven front wings together with spring-coupled passive rear wings. This reduced the number of actuators while retaining coordinated four-wing motion in a compact structure.

Design emphasis

Lightweight structure · simple actuation · four-wing coordination · rapid fabrication and iteration

Prototype development

The project progressed through CAD design, fabrication, assembly, electronics integration, and physical testing. The frame combined 3D-printed components, custom carbon rods, and commercial hardware, with compact onboard control and servo actuation.

Competition result

The project was submitted by team Jiangnan Machinery Factory and received Second Prize in the 31st Zhejiang University Student Mechanical Design Competition.
