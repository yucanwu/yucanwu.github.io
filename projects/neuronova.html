Overview

The project explored an ultra-small wireless battery-free implantable neurochip concept for assistive control, with a focus on people affected by ALS, spinal-cord injury, and severe motor impairment. It received a Silver Award in the International Track of the 2025 China International College Students' Innovation Competition.

My contribution

  • Served as one of the team's major contributors.
  • Focused primarily on hardware enclosure design and fabrication.
  • Used SolidWorks, 3D printing, and commercial components.
