Lunar Lava Tube Exploration Robot

Mechanical concept development for a mobile robot intended for lunar lava-tube exploration.

Space RoboticsSolidWorks3D PrintingCarbon FiberGimbal

Overview

This project explored a mobile robotic platform for lunar lava-tube environments and was associated with Zhejiang University's Qizhen Wenxue program and the 2025 Dandelion Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition preliminary round.

My contribution

  • Contributed to the overall mechanical configuration intended for lunar operating conditions.
  • Designed the top-side component layout.
  • Designed and assembled the camera gimbal.
  • Produced SolidWorks designs for 3D-printed and carbon-fiber components.
