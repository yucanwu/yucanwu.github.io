About & capabilities

Research & engineering interests

My strongest experience is in building electromechanical systems from concept to prototype. Across student research and engineering teams, I have worked on mechanical configuration, CAD, simulation, fabrication, assembly, wiring, debugging, motor control, ROS 2 integration, and system-level technical planning.

I am especially drawn to engineering problems at the technological frontier. My current hands-on experience is strongest in robotics and electromechanical systems, particularly mechanism design, sensing, actuation, control, and hardware–software integration. More broadly, I am eager to explore frontier engineered systems across fields such as advanced energy, aerospace, quantum and photonic technologies, computational imaging and displays, scientific instrumentation, and other emerging technologies that push the boundaries of what engineered systems can do.
