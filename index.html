About & capabilities

How I like to work

My strongest experience is in building electromechanical systems from concept to prototype. Across student research and engineering teams, I have worked on mechanical configuration, CAD, simulation, fabrication, assembly, wiring, debugging, motor control, ROS 2 integration, and system-level technical planning.

I am especially interested in robotics, mechatronics, field/autonomous systems, advanced mechanical systems, and other engineering problems where software and hardware meet the physical world.
